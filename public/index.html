FAQ

Questions before you book?

Can I book for more than one person?

Yes. Add the other applicants during the booking process and the total updates automatically.

When are appointments available?

Weekdays are shown from 6 PM to 9 PM and weekends from 11 AM to 7 PM.

Is this an official government/NIN service?

No. NIN Swindon is an independent appointment-assistance business and is not a government website.

Is payment taken online?

The current version sends the booking request to the team. Payment is handled separately.

Privacy notice

We collect the information entered in the booking form, including name, date of birth, email address and applicant details, to arrange and manage your appointment. Booking information is stored in the business booking database and sent to the booking team for processing.

We do not ask for passwords or payment-card details through this form. Booking information should only be retained for as long as it is reasonably needed to operate the service.
